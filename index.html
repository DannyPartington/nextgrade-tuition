Our Tutors - More Than Just a Teacher

With NextGrade, you’re not just booking a tutor — you’re bringing an expert onboard your child’s educational journey who genuinely cares about their well-being, progress and future.

At NextGrade we only select tutors who have demonstrated they know what it takes to succeed by having achieved a minimum of a grade 8 in the relevant subject. We also make sure our tutors are people that can build trust, encouragement and real relationships with the families they support.

  • We match tutors to your child’s personality, not just their subject.
  • Our tutors have all achieved top grades themselves and know exactly what it takes to succeed.
  • Direct, round-the-clock access to tutors for guidance outside of lessons when needed.
  • We build genuine relationships that boost confidence, motivation and long-term progress.

Founder’s note

NextGrade is built on the idea that tutoring should offer more than just teaching. A great tutor should be a trusted guide, a mentor, a confidant and a strong academic link between home and learning. I want to make this level of service more accessible and affordable for families everywhere.

Through my own experiences in education both as a student and teacher, I came to realise that the classroom is often far from the ideal learning environment. But with the right support, focused progress can be made that achieves far more than school alone.

The goal is simple: low cost, accessible high quality support.
